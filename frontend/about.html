ABOUT US

A simple thought
became Todards.

In a world full of information, I often found it hard to keep up — not because I didn’t care, but because there was just too much to go through.

I wanted a simpler way to stay updated on what’s happening around the world without getting overwhelmed. So, I built Todards.

Todards brings you the most important stories from around the world, distilled into simple, easy-to-read cards — so you can stay informed, even on your busiest days.

A clearer today for a more informed tomorrow.
